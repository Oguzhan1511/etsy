Privacy Policy

PrintySell – Last Updated: August 18, 2026 · Effective Date: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PrintySell ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by the PrintySell team, collects, uses, and protects information gathered through:

  • The PrintySell – Etsy SEO Analyzer Chrome Extension
  • The PrintySell web application at printysell.com

1. What Data We Collect

Chrome Extension:

  • When you visit an Etsy product listing page, the extension reads publicly visible product data from that page (title, tags, view count, etc.) solely to provide SEO analysis.
  • We do not collect your browsing history, personal information, passwords, or financial data.
  • The AI prompt you optionally write is temporarily stored in your browser's chrome.storage.local and sent to the PrintySell API to generate a design. It is not persisted on our servers.

Web Application:

  • Account registration data (e-mail address, hashed password).
  • Etsy OAuth tokens — used only to perform actions on your behalf (e.g. uploading a listing). Tokens are stored encrypted and are never shared.
  • AI prompts and generated image URLs associated with your account session.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the core features of the extension and web application.
  • To authenticate your account and authorize Etsy API calls on your behalf.
  • To generate AI designs using the prompts you provide.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your Etsy passwords or payment details.
  • We do not track or store your browsing history.
  • We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our platform:

  • OpenAI (DALL-E 3 / GPT) – AI image and text generation. Prompts are sent to OpenAI's API according to their Privacy Policy.
  • Etsy API – For reading listing data and publishing products on your behalf.
  • Printify API – For creating and publishing print-on-demand products.
  • Vercel – Web application hosting.

5. Data Retention

  • Extension-local storage data is cleared when you uninstall the extension or clear browser data.
  • Account data is retained as long as your account exists. You may request deletion at any time.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords, and secure token storage. However, no system is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

PrintySell is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Your Rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.
  • You may disconnect your Etsy account integration from within the app settings.
  • You may uninstall the Chrome extension at any time, which removes all locally stored extension data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
